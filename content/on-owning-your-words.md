Social platforms are rented rooms. The landlord can change the rules, the
algorithm, or the locks. My own domain is the only place on the internet where
I make the rules.

## The deal

A personal site costs about $10/year for the domain and $0 for hosting. In
exchange:

1. Your words live at a URL you control
2. No feed, no engagement metrics, no notifications
3. You can change the design with a few lines of CSS

That's the whole pitch. It's the oldest kind of website there is, and it still
works.
